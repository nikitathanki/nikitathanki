Asetonix is a web-based **Enterprise Asset Management and Asset Intelligence Platform** designed to help organizations manage, track, analyze, and optimize their assets throughout their lifecycle.

### Core areas

* Asset Management
* Employee Management
* Asset Assignment
* Asset Return
* Asset Transfers
* Maintenance Management
* Asset Retirement
* Asset Lifecycle
* Audit Trail
* Categories
* Brands
* Models
* Departments
* Locations
* Alerts
* Asset Health
* Utilization Analysis
* Risk Analysis
* Cost Analysis
* Users & Roles
* Dashboard
* Reports
* CSV Data Export

### Future direction

Asetonix is being developed beyond a basic CRUD application toward an **Enterprise Asset Intelligence Platform**.

```text
Asset Data
    ↓
Analytics
    ↓
AI Insights
    ↓
Risk Prediction
    ↓
Predictive Maintenance
    ↓
Anomaly Detection
    ↓
Recommendations
    ↓
Business Decisions
```

Planned areas include:

* AI-powered asset insights
* Predictive maintenance
* Predictive risk analysis
* Anomaly detection
* Asset replacement recommendations
* Total Cost of Ownership analysis
* Advanced business intelligence
* Interactive dashboards
* Advanced data visualization
* Interactive 3D asset visualization
* Workflow and approval automation
* Intelligent alerts
